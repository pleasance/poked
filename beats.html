Tubepop Beat

Fire in time with the music to build your combo. Little friends climb the tube on the beat. Keep every one of them off the rim.

Arrows / A D move around the rim   Space fire   X zap (charges on perfect kills)   P pause   M sound. Shots that land on the beat are Perfect, near it Good. Off-beat shots break your combo.
