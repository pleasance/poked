Down the rabbit hole

Hop on every tile to wake the pyramid. Each tile sings its own note. Keep clear of the Queen's cards.

Hop diagonally with the pad, the arrow keys or W A S D (Up goes up-right, Right goes down-right, Down goes down-left, Left goes up-left), or Q E Z C for the four corners. Swipe on the board works too. Step on every tile enough times to change its color. P pauses.
